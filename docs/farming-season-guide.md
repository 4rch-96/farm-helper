# Farming Season official-guide data map

This file records the static mechanics Farm Helper uses from the [Farming Season player guide](https://farmingseason.io/docs). It is a planning reference, not a price feed, account integration, or promise of earnings.

## Quick path

1. Select one of the official Town Hall levels 1–5.
2. Use the guide-backed timing, energy, capacity, and unlock information to understand an alternative.
3. Add dated local evidence only when you need a monetary comparison.

## Operational mechanics

- Planting or harvesting a field, planting or picking fruit, collecting a Mine cart or running the Oil Well, feeding a building, and collecting a day of animal products cost 2 energy each.
- An arena life costs 5 energy.
- Animals eat every 12 hours and die after 24 hours without food.
- Wheat and corn enter the silo; one unit of each makes 10 feed bags.
- The player market opens at Town Hall 2, has a 5% seller fee, supports at most 10 active listings, and returns an unsold listing after 7 days.

## Intentionally unavailable economics

The guide does not publish current seed, crop, animal, building, rod, bait, or energy-pack prices; crop/product payouts; workshop recipes; recipe output rewards; individual order rewards; resource quantities; current reward share; or current market listings.

Therefore Farm Helper does not call a crop, animal, recipe, order, fishing catch, or resource activity “best” until the required dated values have compatible evidence. Recipes such as bread, cheese, butter, and jam, and all order comparisons, remain evidence-pending until their inputs, duration, energy, and reward are sourced.

A public SEASON/USD quote is also unavailable as a reward-conversion input: it can value a known SEASON amount, but it cannot establish how many tokens the game pays.
